Units read as written: i = 100 mg ibuprofen, p = 100 mg paracetamol, a = 1 antibiotic tablet. Times can be written 13h, 13h30, 13:30 or 13.30. Times without a date carry over from the last date line; times going backwards roll to the next day. The log stays on this phone between visits — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Clear when the course is over.

This is a timer, not medical advice. Follow your prescription, and contact your dentist or doctor if pain or swelling gets worse, or if you have kidney, liver, stomach, heart or asthma problems, are pregnant, or drink alcohol daily.
